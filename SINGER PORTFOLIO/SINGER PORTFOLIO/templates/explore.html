Sujata Gurav Kammar

Hindustani Classical Vocalist | Kirana Gharana

Biography

Vidushi Smt. Sujata Gurav Kammar is an accomplished Hindustani classical vocalist associated with the Kirana Gharana tradition. She was born on 19 November 1973 into a family with a strong musical heritage.

She received her initial musical training from her father and guru, Late Pandit Sangameshwar Gurav, a respected musician of the Kirana Gharana tradition. She later continued her musical training under her brother, Pandit Kaivalya Kumar Gurav.

Sujata Gurav Kammar is known for her melodious voice, expressive rendition of ragas, intricate taans and murkis, and strong rhythmic understanding. Along with Hindustani classical music, she has also performed Vachana, Dasavani, Abhang, Thumri and Natya Sangeet, demonstrating her versatility as a vocalist.

Education

  • M.A. in Music, Karnataka University, Dharwad
  • Guest Faculty, Department of Music, Karnataka Arts College, Dharwad

Major Performances

She has performed at several reputed music festivals and cultural platforms across India, including:

  • Hari Vallabh Samaroh, Jalandhar
  • Tansen Sangeet Samaroh, Gwalior
  • Naadam, Kolkata
  • Delhi Karnataka Sangha
  • Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Pune
  • Ritu Basanth, Udaipur
  • Ramanavami Music Festival, Mysuru
  • Sangeetotsava, Dharwad and Hubballi

Awards & Recognition

  • Manik Varma Award – Pune Gayan Samaj
  • Sangeet Vikas Ratna
  • Swara Saraswati Title
  • Vocational Excellence Award – Rotary Club of Dharwad
  • Tappa-Thumri Award – South Central Zone Cultural Centre, Nagpur
  • Dashakada Sadhakaru honour
  • Naada Habba Outstanding Artist Award
  • Avva Award for contribution to music

Musical Style

Her music reflects the depth and aesthetics of the Kirana Gharana, with emphasis on the beauty of individual swaras, melodic development, emotional expression and detailed raga presentation. She is equally at home with traditional classical forms and devotional genres, making her performances deeply spiritual and aesthetically refined.
