Milestones of Excellence: Celebrating the Journey of SUJATA GURAV

Discover the extensive list of awards and recognitions that Vidushi Sujata Gurav Kammar has received at both state and national levels, each serving as a testament to her exceptional contributions to the world of Hindustani classical music. Her illustrious career is marked by numerous accolades that reflect not only her extraordinary talent, but also her profound impact on the music world, affirming her status as a celebrated artist whose music continues to inspire and enchant audiences across India and beyond.

Major Performance Venues

She has performed at several reputed music festivals and cultural platforms across India:

  • Hari Vallabh Samaroh, Jalandhar
  • Tansen Sangeet Samaroh, Gwalior
  • Naadam, Kolkata
  • Delhi Karnataka Sangha
  • Bharatiya Vidya Bhavan, Pune
  • Ritu Basanth, Udaipur
  • Ramanavami Music Festival, Mysuru
  • Sangeetotsava, Dharwad and Hubballi

Awards & Honours

  • Manik Varma Award – Pune Gayan Samaj
  • Sangeet Vikas Ratna
  • Swara Saraswati Title
  • Vocational Excellence Award – Rotary Club, Dharwad
  • Tappa-Thumri Award – South Central Zone Cultural Centre, Nagpur
  • Dashakada Sadhakaru honour
  • Naada Habba Outstanding Artist Award
  • Avva Award for contribution to music
